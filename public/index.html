Faustus Ledger is a free fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Grinding Gear Games. Path of Exile, its item names, icons and game data belong to Grinding Gear Games. Market data comes from GGG's public Currency Exchange API, hourly and at least an hour old.

Free, no ads, no accounts, no cookies, no tracking. Nothing about you is collected. Your settings and stars stay in your own browser. Item icons load from GGG's image server; beast and Recombinator icons are copies of images from the community PoE Wiki.

Prices are estimates from past trades, not advice. Check the live ratio in game before you trade. Open source under the MIT license.
